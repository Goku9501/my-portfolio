PRIVACY POLICY

App: Way 📱 Last updated: October 22, 2026 Mobile App 🔒

This Privacy Policy explains how Way collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Way mobile application. We believe in clear, honest communication about your data.

tl;dr — the highlights
📱
What We Collect

Phone number, profile info, location, photos, device tokens, and anonymised analytics.

🎯
Why We Use It

To authenticate you, power app features, send notifications, and improve the service.

🛡️
Your Control

Manage permissions in device settings. Request deletion anytime via the app or email.

the full details ↓
Phone Number & Authentication Data

Used to verify your identity and provide secure sign-in. Processed through our authentication provider and never sold to third parties.

Profile Information

Name, profile details, and profile photo that you voluntarily provide to personalise your experience.

Location Data

Way collects approximate or precise location (based on your permission) while the app is in the foreground to power location-based features. Background location is only collected if you grant "Always Allow" and only when a feature requires it.

Photos & Uploads

You may upload photos (e.g., profile pictures or in-app content). Files are stored securely on our cloud storage provider and used solely to deliver the feature you requested.

Device & Notification Tokens

Device or push-notification tokens are collected to deliver timely and relevant push notifications to your device.

Analytics & Diagnostics

Crash reports, performance metrics, and anonymised usage data (e.g., feature interactions, session duration) to identify bugs and improve app quality via our analytics and crash-reporting SDKs.

We use collected information to:

  • →Provide & maintain Way — operate core features and keep the app running reliably.
  • →Authenticate users — verify your identity and keep your account secure.
  • →Support app features — power location-based functionality, profile management, uploads, and other in-app features.
  • →Deliver notifications — send push notifications and in-app alerts relevant to your activity.
  • →Protect accounts — detect fraud, abuse, and security threats.
  • →Troubleshoot & improve — analyse diagnostics and usage patterns to fix bugs and enhance the experience.

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by trusted service providers that support the app:

🔧 Backend & Hosting
Cloud infrastructure for app data and API services.
🔐 Authentication
Phone verification and secure sign-in provider.
📦 File Storage
Secure cloud storage for uploaded photos and media.
🔔 Push Notifications
Notification delivery service (e.g., FCM).
📊 Analytics
Usage analytics to understand feature engagement.
🐛 Crash Reporting
Diagnostics provider to detect and resolve app errors.

We may also disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your information. These include:

  • ✓Encryption in transit — all data between the app and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • ✓Encryption at rest — stored data is encrypted using industry-standard methods.
  • ✓Access controls — only authorised personnel and systems can access user data.
  • ✓Secure authentication — phone-number OTP verification reduces account takeover risk.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the Way service and comply with legal obligations.

Retention Periods
Account & profile dataUntil account deletion
Location dataSession / feature use only
Analytics & diagnosticsUp to 24 months
BackupsUp to 90 days post-deletion

Requesting deletion: Use the Delete Account option in app Settings, or email khatrisagar415@gmail.com. Requests are processed within 30 days; backup copies may persist for up to 90 additional days.

You can manage the following permissions in your device's Settings at any time:

  • →Location: Set to "While Using", "Always", or "Never" via Settings → Way. Disabling limits location-based features.
  • →Photos & Media: Control photo library access via Settings → Way → Photos.
  • →Notifications: Toggle push notifications via Settings → Way → Notifications.

To access, correct, or delete your personal information, contact us at khatrisagar415@gmail.com.

Way is intended for users who are 13 years of age or older (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us at khatrisagar415@gmail.com and we will promptly delete that information.

We may update this Privacy Policy when our data practices or legal requirements change. When revised, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

For material changes, we may provide additional notice within the app. Continued use of Way after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
